# Brand Voice Prompt Kit

A way to make AI drafts sound like a specific executive, not a generic marketer.

## The problem

A ghostwriter's job is to disappear. A post only works if the executive reads it and thinks, "That's exactly how I'd say it." AI drafts for founders and executives tend to sound the same: polished, smooth and nobody's. The reader on LinkedIn can tell, and the executive won't post it.

## What this does

A fill-in voice guide. You complete it once for a person, using a short capture call and their real writing. Then you paste it at the top of any drafting prompt.

## How to use it

1. Copy `voice-guide-template.md` and run the capture call with the executive.
2. Fill in the guide from the call and from three real posts or messages.
3. Paste the finished guide above your drafting prompt.
4. Compare the draft to their real writing and update the guide wherever it misses.

## What's inside

- `voice-guide-template.md`: the template, including the capture call questions
